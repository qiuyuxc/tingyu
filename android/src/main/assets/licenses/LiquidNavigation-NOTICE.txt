Files adapted into com.qiuyuxc.tingyu.navigation:
LiquidGlassNavigationBar.kt, DampedDragAnimation.kt, InteractiveHighlight.kt,
CombinedBackdrop.kt, InnerShadow.kt, Lens.kt and Vibrancy.kt.

Modified for Tingyu on 2026-10-28: package integration, app navigation items,
theme lookup, font-scaled sizing, safe-area handling and accessible navigation.
The effect implementation is only entered on Android 13 and later.

Further modified in 0.4.0: restore full upstream backdrop recording and tinted-tab
sampling, gravity-driven highlights, press spot, moving refraction with dispersion,
inner shadow, rubber-band movement and velocity deformation. Font-scaled heights
and outer safe insets remain. Hidden sampling passes do not duplicate keyboard or
accessibility targets. Drag jobs are coalesced and animation velocity is read directly.

Further modified in 0.4.1: pointer movement updates the indicator and panel offset
directly. Spring animations run after release, retaining gesture velocity and the
full glass effects. Cancelled gestures restore the previous selected tab.

This is the Apache-licensed upstream example also referenced by KernelSU's
FloatingBottomBar. KernelSU source was inspected as a visual/integration reference;
its application implementation was not copied.

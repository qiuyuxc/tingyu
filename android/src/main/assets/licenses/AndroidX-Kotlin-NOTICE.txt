AndroidX, Jetpack Compose and Kotlin runtime components

AndroidX and Jetpack Compose are Android Open Source Project libraries.
Upstream: https://github.com/androidx/androidx

Kotlin standard-library and JetBrains annotation components are published by JetBrains.
Upstream: https://github.com/JetBrains/kotlin
Upstream: https://github.com/JetBrains/java-annotations

kotlinx.coroutines is published by the Kotlin project.
Upstream: https://github.com/Kotlin/kotlinx.coroutines
